TL;DR: Node’s standalone binaries have been broken on major Linux distributions since version 25. Stay on 24 if you need them functional by default.

Very odd that this is being treated as “as designed” instead of as a major regression.

https://github.com/nodejs/node/blob/v25.2.0/BUILDING.md#official-binary-platforms-and-toolchains
